PikaSpace includes six wallpaper photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), verified as free for personal and commercial use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The images are cropped, resized, compressed as WebP, and bundled locally with matching previews. They are not fetched from Unsplash during dashboard use.

Photo credits, original source links, adaptations, and license details are included in [public/wallpapers/CREDITS.txt](public/wallpapers/CREDITS.txt) and **Settings → Wallpaper → Photo credits & licenses**. The photographers are Priscilla Du Preez, Luca Bravo, Lukasz Szmigiel, Diego Jimenez, and Jeremy Bishop; the Cosmos image credits NASA, ESA, and F. Summers, J. DePasquale, and D. Player (STScI).

PikaSpace 1.0 bundles React, React DOM, Scheduler, and Lucide icons. Full installed runtime dependency notices are generated into `extension/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` during the build.
